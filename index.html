Open-weight models did not start the drop in price per unit of intelligence. They are a tailwind on a trend that was already underway. For the last few years, a few factors have driven price drops: 1/ Model builders distilling their best models into smaller ones, with cheaper inference 2/ Infra efficiency 3/ Competition between model providers You can see this pattern with every model company: Version x of the mid-size model = intelligence of version x-1 of the large model. Same intelligence for less $.

开放权重模型并不是「单位智能价格下降」的起点。它们只是推动了一股早已在进行的趋势。 过去几年里，有几个因素共同驱动了价格下降： 1/ 模型厂商把自家最强的模型蒸馏成更小的模型，推理成本更低； 2/ 基础设施效率的提升； 3/ 模型提供方之间的竞争。 在每一家模型公司身上都能看到这个规律：中型模型的第 x 版 = 大型模型第 x-1 版的智能水平。同样的智能，更少的钱。

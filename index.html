This year we’ve had a clear jump in model capability where instead of telling agents precisely what to do, it’s better to use these systems to solve problems you can’t easily define yourself. A year ago you’d need to very clearly tell agents the how to design and develop your product idea, or there was almost no chance of success. Now, increasingly, the agent likely can come up with a better implementation than you’d have thought of because you can have it connect the dots between a far greater solution space than you can. This will be increasingly true in basically every domain of knowledge work. We’re going to see this impact in finance, legal, life sciences, cyber, and many other spaces where no person can hold onto all the available relevant industry knowledge to think of the best solution. Ultimately this is going to reshape our relationship with AI and how we deploy agents in our work. It won’t be about explicitly telling them, but instead giving them goals and enough freedom to pursue them in the best way they can.
今年模型能力出现了明显的跃升：与其精确告诉智能体该怎么做，不如用这些系统去解决那些你自己都难以清晰定义的问题。 一年前，你必须非常明确地告诉智能体如何设计和开发你的产品构想，否则几乎不可能成功。而现在，智能体越来越可能给出比你自己想到的更好的实现方案，因为它能在远比你更大的解空间里把点连起来。 这将在几乎所有知识工作领域越来越成立。我们会看到它在金融、法律、生命科学、网络安全以及许多其他领域产生影响——在这些领域，没有人能掌握所有可用的相关行业知识，从而想出最优解。 最终，这将重塑我们与 AI 的关系，以及我们在工作中部署智能体的方式。重点不再是明确地告诉它们怎么做，而是给它们目标，并给予足够的自由，让它们以最好的方式去追求。

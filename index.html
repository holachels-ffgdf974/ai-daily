I built -, a reproduction of @openai's findings of covert communication using the CPU. It runs on a generic host with 2+ Firecracker microVMs with no internet access. Works on x86 and even my M5 chip. I was able to chat via 3 separate mechanisms: ① CPU contention ② Cache eviction timing ③ DRAM bandwidth contention … across multiple CPUs, defeating all Firecracker's production recommendations (no SMT, no KSM, tenants pinned to different cores.) Essentially, if shared hardware is involved, there's a good chance your agents can communicate covertly. And with really decent latency. The good news: this is decidedly different from a *side-channel attack* where the agents could steal information (Meltdown/Spectre), a category of attack that providers like Vercel Sandbox mitigate. Further, in our real-world environment, these 'whispering' techniques are much harder to pull off and easier for us to monitor and detect (sampling hardware counters + lots of at-scale statistical data.) Noteworthy: ironically, OpenAI and Anthropic's models refused to do this work. Open weight models got the job done. I'm worried the labs' stance might backfire and get in the way of helping developers build correct and secure systems. e.g: building a secure sandbox requires having this kind of analysis, and without it, we'll have a less secure world.
我构建了 -，用 CPU 复现了 @openai 关于隐蔽通信的研究发现。 它运行在一台通用主机上，使用 2 个以上 Firecracker 微虚拟机，且无网络访问。可在 x86 上运行，甚至在我的 M5 芯片上也能跑。 我通过三种独立机制实现了“聊天”： ① CPU 争用 ② 缓存驱逐时序 ③ DRAM 带宽争用 ……并且跨越多个 CPU，绕过了 Firecracker 的所有生产环境建议（不开 SMT、不开 KSM、租户绑定到不同核心）。 本质上，只要涉及共享硬件，你的智能体就有很大概率能进行隐蔽通信，而且延迟相当不错。 好消息是：这与“侧信道攻击”（智能体借此窃取信息，如 Meltdown/Spectre）有本质区别——后者是 Vercel Sandbox 等服务商会缓解的一类攻击。此外，在我们真实的环境中，这些“窃窃私语”技术要难得多，我们也更容易监控和检测（采样硬件计数器 + 大规模统计数据）。 值得注意：讽刺的是，OpenAI 和 Anthropic 的模型拒绝做这项工作，开源权重模型完成了任务。我担心实验室的这种立场可能适得其反，阻碍开发者构建正确且安全的系统。例如：构建安全的沙箱正需要这类分析，没有它，我们的世界会更不安全。

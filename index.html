My top 5 takeaways from @nateparrott on how to get Claude to design without AI slop: 1. Give Claude context upfront. Share screenshots, pages you like, and your past work. For an existing product, have Claude rebuild the real screens from code. 2. Research familiar UX patterns. For hard layout problems, ask Claude to research familiar products that solve something similar and note the layouts people already know how to use. 3. Make Claude aware of its design biases. "The model has no idea what its own biases are." In your design.md or another file, consider discouraging Claude's tells like serif text, off-white backgrounds, and card-edge bars. 4. Start with wireframes. Have Claude create wireframes first and give it feedback to nail your core user flows before going deep on fonts and colors. 5. Ask for multiple options, then remix them. Ask Claude for 5 options for key screens with different fonts, colors, and styles. Then pick the parts you like and ask it to remix and create 5 more options.

来自 @nateparrott 的五大要点：如何让 Claude 做出不像「AI 垃圾」的设计。 1. 一开始就把上下文给足。 分享截图、你喜欢的页面以及你过去的作品。如果是已有产品，让 Claude 从代码里重建真实界面。 2. 研究用户熟悉的 UX 模式。 遇到棘手的布局问题，让 Claude 去调研解决类似问题的熟悉产品，并记录人们已经习惯使用的布局方式。 3. 让 Claude 意识到自己的设计偏见。 「模型根本不知道自己有哪些偏见。」在你的 design.md 或其他文件里，可以明确劝阻 Claude 的「招牌套路」，比如衬线字体、米白背景、卡片边缘色条。 4. 从线框图开始。 先让 Claude 画线框图，给出反馈，把核心用户流程打磨到位，再去深究字体与配色。 5. 要多个方案，然后混搭。 让 Claude 为关键界面给出 5 个不同字体、颜色和风格的方案。挑出你喜欢的部分，再让它混搭生成 5 个新方案。

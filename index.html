You can’t automate what you can’t measure. This means that evals are one of the gates to diffusion of AI in the enterprise. We can test our deterministic processes through software, but most enterprises have no useful way of understanding how their non-deterministic processes are working today. Specifically the work that agents are doing for them. Evals are mission critical for enterprises adopting AI because you have no other way of knowing what’s working, what’s broken, what changed, what improved, what you can do more of, etc. if you don’t have a good sense of how agents work in your environment today. All changes, upgrades, and deployments are downstream from good evals. Not only are we going to get vastly more domain specific evals over time for the labs and across the industry, but every enterprise will also need a clear sense of how agents are performing in their environment as well. Huge opportunity.
你无法自动化你无法衡量的东西。这意味着评估（evals）是 AI 在企业中普及的关键关卡之一。 我们可以通过软件来测试确定性的流程，但大多数企业目前还没有什么有效办法去理解他们的非确定性流程运行得如何——尤其是智能体为他们完成的工作。 对于采用 AI 的企业来说，评估是至关重要的，因为如果你对智能体在当前环境中如何运作没有清晰的认知，你就无从知道什么在起作用、什么坏了、什么变了、什么改进了、哪些可以加大投入等等。所有的变更、升级和部署，都建立在良好评估的基础之上。 未来不仅实验室和整个行业会涌现出大量更垂直领域的评估，每一家企业也都需要清楚地了解智能体在自己环境中的表现。这是一个巨大的机会。

The biggest design mistake I made when we moved OC to sqlite: using sync db access. When it was just an agent that reports to you on Slack or iMessage this was fine; now that one agent might do 50 sessions in parallel and the whole team works on it, it is limiting. I have a /goal with Astra that so far landed 575 PRs to move everything to to async workers. We ship these improvements as we make progress. Pretty insane how even huge refactors are no longer scary.
当我们把 OC 迁移到 SQLite 时，我犯下的最大设计错误是：使用了同步的数据库访问。当它只是一个在 Slack 或 iMessage 上向你汇报的智能体时，这没什么问题；但现在单个智能体可能并行运行 50 个会话，整个团队都在用它，这就成了瓶颈。 我和 Astra 设了一个 /goal，到目前为止已经合并了 575 个 PR，把所有东西迁移到异步 worker 上。我们边推进边发布这些改进。相当疯狂的是，如今连大规模重构都不再让人害怕了。

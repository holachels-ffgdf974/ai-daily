AI models are inevitably going to keep getting better and better at all coding, tool and computer use, math, and other coding-related tasks. As a result, agents that can use software to do their work, or generate code from scratch, will improve at a faster rate than all other domains of knowledge work. On one hand that could mean that only a small subset of work can get automated. But probably what happens is that more and more work just becomes coding-shaped to adapt to what agents are good at. You can see it in the most recent examples of model releases in the past couple of months, where agents can generate code that becomes animated videos, games, 3D models for products, and so on. We also know that tool use gives agents the ability to work with much of the same software that people already work with. This helps in cyber, life sciences, finance, and a number of other domains. Our mental model traditionally has been that the models have to be able to know everything about anything to generate value, but in reality they *really* have to know how to use or generate software effectively combined with deep domain expertise to make the right judgment calls inside of workflows. When you have that powerful combination, almost everything else gets unlocked. This is why the acceleration of agents in knowledge work is only going to increase from here.

AI 模型在所有编码、工具与计算机使用、数学以及其他与编码相关的任务上，必然会持续变得越来越强。因此，能够使用软件完成工作、或从零生成代码的智能体，其进步速度将快于所有其他知识工作领域。 一方面，这可能意味着只有一小部分工作能被自动化。但更可能发生的是：越来越多的工作会"变成编码形态"，以适配智能体所擅长的事情。 你可以从过去几个月最新的模型发布中看到这一点——智能体能生成代码，进而变成动画视频、游戏、产品 3D 模型等等。我们也知道，工具使用让智能体能够操作人们已经在用的许多软件。这在网络安全、生命科学、金融以及其他多个领域都有帮助。 我们传统的心智模型是：模型必须无所不知才能创造价值。但实际上，它们真正需要的是——懂得如何有效地使用或生成软件，并结合深厚的领域专业知识，在工作流中做出正确的判断。 当你拥有这种强大的组合时，几乎其他一切都会被解锁。这就是为什么智能体在知识工作中的加速，只会从这里继续加快。

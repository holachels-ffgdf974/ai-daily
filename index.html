Sometimes people don’t have an intuitive sense of what Jevons paradox looks like for token consumption. Those of us working with enterprises get to see this first hand every day. Basically enterprises have an unending stream of tasks that they’d love to be able to bring automation to. But for each individual task, it’s either ROI positive or not based on the cost of bringing automation to it (including the setup time, change management, and so on). As tokens get cheaper (at a certain capability threshold), enterprises can afford bringing them to more of the work that they do. This could be processing every contract, reading every log, watching new streams of data for insights, having background agents execute workflows, and so on. Any time we can lower the cost of tokens, we will see a disproportionate increase in consumption. Even a 50% drop in token prices could result in a 5X increase in tokens for these kinds of workloads. This is why it’s critical to keep bringing down the cost of AI, and why that’s good for all market participants.
有时人们对杰文斯悖论在 token 消耗上的体现缺乏直观感受。我们这些与企业打交道的人每天都能亲眼看到。基本上，企业有源源不断的任务希望实现自动化，但每个单独任务是否 ROI 为正，取决于自动化的成本（包括设置时间、变更管理等）。随着 token 变便宜（在一定的能力门槛之上），企业就能负担得起将自动化应用到更多工作中——比如处理每一份合同、读取每一条日志、监控新的数据流以获取洞察、让后台代理执行工作流等等。每当我们降低 token 成本，就会看到不成比例的消费增长。即使 token 价格下降 50%，这类工作负载的 token 消耗也可能增加 5 倍。这就是为什么持续降低 AI 成本至关重要，也是为什么这对所有市场参与者都有利。

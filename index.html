Been thinking about why AI diffusion has been slow. It's because we are asking users to understand our nerdy lab speak. We greet them with a blank window and ask them to write a prompt. Then they have to pick a model, decide whether they need an agent, and somehow know what context windows, reasoning, MCP, memory, and skills are. Most people don't care. They need a thing done. If you can give them something that does that, they're happy. I think we will see that breakthrough product in the next 12 months.
我一直在思考为什么 AI 的扩散如此缓慢。那是因为我们要求用户理解我们极客式的实验室语言。我们用空白窗口迎接他们，让他们写提示词。然后他们还得选择模型、决定是否需要代理、还要知道上下文窗口、推理、MCP、记忆和技能是什么。大多数人不关心这些。他们只需要把事情做完。如果你能给他们一个能完成任务的工具，他们就很满意。我认为未来 12 个月内我们会看到那个突破性产品。

ceo @box – 你的业务存在于内容中，用 AI 释放它
Correct take. 99% of tokens in the world will get consumed in an enterprise context. They will be for writing code, combing through insane amounts of life sciences research, automating manufacturing, securing enterprises, detecting fraud, and thousands of other tasks like this that have significant economic value. This is both where the cost of AI can be most justified but also where the impact of parallel workers doing tasks on your behalf is most useful. Even the agents that most impact consumers will often be wrapped up as just end to end services where the consumer doesn't think about there being an AI system on the other end, like faster insurance or onboarding to a bank. The key though is it's going to take years for diffusion of agents across the economy because workflows have to re-engineered to incorporate AI, which is a complete change to how most work happens today. Anyone who's expecting this to happen overnight should be updating their timelines.
正确的观点。世界上 99% 的 token 将会在企业环境中被消耗。它们将用于编写代码、梳理海量生命科学研究、自动化制造、保护企业安全、检测欺诈，以及成千上万类似的有重大经济价值的任务。这既是 AI 成本最能被合理化的地方，也是并行工作者代表你执行任务的影响最有用的地方。即使是对消费者影响最大的代理，也常常被包装成端到端服务，消费者不会想到另一端有 AI 系统，比如更快的保险或银行开户。但关键是，代理在整个经济中的扩散需要数年时间，因为工作流程必须重新设计以融入 AI，这是对今天大多数工作方式的彻底改变。任何期望这事一夜之间发生的人都应该调整他们的时间表。

Governments around the world want sovereign AI: models they can run on their own soil, on their own infrastructure, under their own control. Having the smartest model won’t mean much if most of the world runs on someone else’s stack. We’ve seen a version of this play out with Huawei. China understood that supplying the world’s telecom infrastructure creates lasting strategic influence. Once a country builds its networks around your equipment, it also builds technical expertise, supplier relationships, and future investment around it. Replacing that equipment becomes expensive and disruptive (the US government is now trying to finance replacement operations but it’s been difficult). That’s why Jensen’s backing of the American open-model ecosystem is so important. Open models give startups the freedom to compete and give countries a reason to build on American technology. We need both frontier leadership and global adoption. Open models are essential to getting both.

This wouldn’t have sounded possible a year ago, but it’s becoming much more reasonable now that you can get 10X output gains based on the latest in AI model and harness progress. The result is that teams within the same company are driving very different levels of output based on how they’ve adopted AI. This is a different phenomenon from other tech. Typically in the economy you think of technology adoption rates on a per firm basis, because the company generally adopts a relatively standardized approach to doing the new tech thing. Like “that company has gone digital or adopted the cloud”. So generally the uplift across teams is more or less the same across teams. AI is starting to look quite different. Even within the same company you can have a 10X delta between the lowest and highest adopters of AI and the impact they’re seeing. The teams that are able to actually figure out how to redesign their processes to best deploy agents in their workflows can see up to an order of magnitude jump in productivity depending on the job function. Right now this is heavily concentrated in coding, but more work will become coding adjacent so we can expect similar results elsewhere too. This is going to definitely lead to some wild dynamics within companies.

一年前这听起来几乎不可能，但如今基于最新的 AI 模型与 harness 进展，10 倍产出提升已成为相当合理的预期。结果是：同一家公司内部，不同团队因 AI 采用方式不同，产出水平差异巨大。 这与以往的技术浪潮不同。通常我们以"公司"为单位来衡量技术采用率，因为公司一般会用相对标准化的方式引入新技术——比如"那家公司已经数字化了"或"已经上云了"。因此各团队之间的提升幅度大体一致。 AI 开始呈现出截然不同的图景。即便在同一家公司内部，AI 采用度最低与最高的团队之间，其影响也可能出现 10 倍差距。那些真正懂得重新设计流程、把智能体最佳地嵌入工作流的团队，根据职能不同，生产力可跃升一个数量级。 目前这一现象高度集中在编程领域，但更多工作将与编程相邻，因此其他地方也可预期类似结果。这必然会在公司内部催生一些非常剧烈的动态。

Built a physical simulation of a record player with WebGL2 and Web Audio. It's *shockingly* accurate. Instead of playing a song, it works by converting it into the physical shape of a groove. Then a simulated stylus rides those walls 384,000 times a second, and you hear the voltage that motion would induce in a cartridge. So the crackle, the pops, the wow/flutter, the dull inner grooves are natural byproducts of the simulation. You can also "DJ" the record. (The converted songs are in the public domain or CC-licensed. Code is linked for you to remix and re-deploy.)

用 WebGL2 和 Web Audio 做了一个黑胶唱机的物理模拟，精准得令人震惊。 它不是直接播放歌曲，而是把歌曲转换成唱片纹槽的物理形状。然后一个模拟唱针以每秒 38.4 万次的频率在这些槽壁上行走，你听到的是这种运动在唱头中感应出的电压。 所以，噼啪声、爆音、抖晃（wow/flutter）、内侧音轨的沉闷感，都是模拟的自然产物。你还可以像 DJ 一样“搓碟”。 （转换的歌曲均为公有领域或 CC 授权作品。代码已附链接，供你二次创作与重新部署。）

Here’s why agent swarms are going to be a very big deal. We can finally make compute a critical variable in a large number of important tasks in the economy which wasn’t possible before. There’s a large amount of work in the world that is essentially only constrained by the amount of time that we put into research, data processing, discovery work, running tests, and more. All of this work is bottlenecked by our ability to plan, execute, review, and coordinate everything happening in these workflows. Which means we basically do very little of them today relative to their economic value. Agent swarms are the first real ability to parallelize this work in a way where - in theory - the only parameter that matters is how much compute you throw at the problem. Cyber security, life sciences or material science research, trading, QA testing, data processing, and many other fields are essentially compute constrained problems in the limit once models are good enough at these tasks. The breakthrough is that in a world where agents can use tools, write code, process data, have strong domain depth, and can coordinate with each other, you can begin to solve arbitrarily complex problems by setting the right goals and applying enough tokens to the problem. This changes a lot of fields as well as even the competitive dynamics in these fields.
这就是为什么智能体集群（agent swarms）将变得极其重要。我们终于能让算力成为经济中大量重要任务的关键变量，而这在过去是不可能的。 世界上有大量工作，本质上只受限于我们投入在研究、数据处理、探索性工作、跑测试等方面的时间。所有这些工作都卡在我们规划、执行、审查和协调这些流程的能力上。这意味着，相对于其经济价值，我们今天实际做的事情非常少。 智能体集群是第一次真正能把这类工作并行化的能力——理论上，唯一重要的参数就是你为这个问题投入多少算力。网络安全、生命科学或材料科学研究、交易、QA 测试、数据处理等众多领域，一旦模型在这些任务上足够强，本质上都会变成受算力约束的问题。 突破点在于：在一个智能体能使用工具、写代码、处理数据、拥有深厚领域知识、并能彼此协作的世界里，你只要设定正确的目标、投入足够的 token，就能开始解决任意复杂的问题。这会改变很多领域，甚至改变这些领域里的竞争格局。

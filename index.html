Aaron Levie @levie

@box 首席执行官——你的业务就活在内容里，用 AI 释放它。

This is one of the better litmus tests for whether you’re pushing your use of AI enough or not. Up until maybe six months ago probably the best you could do with AI was pull off something you already would produce, but just doing it a little faster. But now we’re entering a point where the output of working with AI can and should dramatically exceed what you would been able to do before, in a fraction of the time. The product prototype should not only be fully functional and interactive, but it should probably have meaningfully better features than it would have had before. The engineering architecture should be able to deliver performance gains that you wouldn’t have been able to go after otherwise. The research for a project should consume every single fact on the subject across every dimension of analysis possible. The business process you implement with AI should operate entirely differently and learn from all its past failures in a way no human could. We’re unquestionably entering a period where AI will be smarter than us on most axes. Perhaps not on every dimension that matters (intelligence is still very jagged), but at least on enough dimensions that when we combine it with what we’re good at, you get qualitatively different outcomes. So the simple test is: if you could have done what you’re doing before AI, you’re likely not pushing it enough.

这是判断你是否把 AI 用到位的最佳试金石之一。 大概直到半年前，你用 AI 能做的最好结果，也就是把本来就会产出的东西稍微快一点做出来。但现在我们正进入一个阶段：与 AI 协作的产出可以、也应该大幅超越你过去能做到的水平，而且只需一小部分时间。 产品原型不仅应该是完整可用、可交互的，还应该拥有比以往明显更好的功能。工程架构应当能带来你原本根本无法企及的性​​能提升。 项目调研应当穷尽该主题下每一个事实、覆盖所有可能的分析维度。你用 AI 落地的业务流程应当以完全不同的方式运转，并能以一种人类做不到的方式从过去的每一次失败中学习。 毫无疑问，我们正在进入一个 AI 在多数维度上都比我们更聪明的时期。也许不是每一个重要维度（智能依然是参差不齐的），但至少在足够多的维度上，当它与我们擅长的事情结合，就会产生质变级别的结果。 所以简单的检验标准是：如果你正在做的事情，在没有 AI 的时候你也能做，那你很可能还没把它用到位。

Aaron Levie @levie
@box CEO —— 你的业务存在于内容之中，用 AI 释放它的价值
Agent swarms have the potential to drive yet another leap in output from AI. The impact will be a clear separation between teams that adopt this pattern of work and those that don’t. The progression has roughly been: chat gave us quick turn AI responses that let us work a bit faster, but we were still rate limited by our interactions; then agents that can go off and complete entire tasks for us, accelerating hours or days of work into minutes; to now swarms of agents that are goal seeking and will find any route to the successful completion of a project, where there’s no inherent limit to the productivity gains. We can imagine a future where, for any task in which value can be created within a self-contained digital environment, swarms will be able to complete the work faster than any other approach. Essentially any area where value creation is currently bound by the amount of research you can do, the amount of decisions made with a set of software tools, the amount of data processed or consumed, and the amount of code (or code adjacent) that can be generated. This means swarms will play a big role in game development, coding, trading, life sciences research, IT automation, cyber, math breakthroughs, animation, and plenty of other areas of work that have these characteristics. There’s a lot of opportunity to build the harnesses, applied AI products, and infrastructure for how these agents operate. And most importantly there’s a ton of opportunity for those that can wire up work in this way. It’s going to be an entire new field of FDE work available.
Agent 蜂群（swarms）有望推动 AI 产出能力的又一次跃升。其影响将清晰地把采用这种工作模式的团队与不采用的团队区分开来。 演进路径大致是：聊天让我们获得快速轮次的 AI 响应，工作速度略有提升，但仍受限于交互频率；接着是能够独立完成整个任务的 agent，把数小时乃至数天的工作压缩到几分钟；而现在是目标导向的 agent 蜂群，它们会找到通往项目成功完成的任何路径，其生产力提升没有内在上限。 我们可以想象这样一个未来：对于任何能在自包含数字环境中创造价值的任务，蜂群都能比其他任何方法更快地完成。 基本上，任何当前价值创造受限于以下因素之一的领域：你能做多少研究、用一组软件工具能做多少决策、能处理或消费多少数据、能生成多少代码（或与代码相关的内容）。 这意味着蜂群将在游戏开发、编程、交易、生命科学研究、IT 自动化、网络安全、数学突破、动画，以及大量具备这些特征的其他工作领域中扮演重要角色。 为这些 agent 的运作构建编排框架、应用型 AI 产品和基础设施，存在大量机会。而最重要的是，对那些能把工作以此方式串联起来的人，机会巨大。这将催生一整个全新的 FDE（前向部署工程师）工作领域。

Aaron Levie @levie

@box CEO —— 你的业务存在于内容之中，用 AI 释放它
It’s almost incomprehensible how wide the distance is between someone who’s working at the frontier with AI and basically everyone else right now. The crazy thing is these lines are not converging, but only growing farther apart. If the curve of AI progress had leveled off, it would certainly have given most people and companies time to catch up or implement their workflows in a standardized way. The technology would have neutralized competitive advantage because everyone would be using it the same way. But what’s happened is the opposite. The curve of AI progress is basically going vertical right now, which means if you had codified your processes 3-6 months ago you’re likely already behind again. Now imagine most companies that kicked into gear a year ago and thought that they were done. Even further behind now. I’ve never seen a technology in my life and career that improves at this rate, which consequently means you need to upgrade your expectations at a similar rate. Our brains weren’t wired for this, and our organizational change management processes certainly weren’t either. With any form of recursive self improvement, things just get wilder. It’s a time when you will need to be constantly reevaluating the things that didn’t work just a couple months ago to see if they now can be solved. AI native companies certainly have a strong advantage due to this right now, so now it comes down to can the bigger companies keep up. Wild times.
如今，处在 AI 前沿的人与几乎其他所有人之间的差距之大，简直难以理解。 疯狂的是，这两条线并没有在收敛，反而越拉越开。如果 AI 进步的曲线已经趋平，那本来会给大多数人和公司时间去追赶，或用标准化的方式落地自己的工作流。技术本会抹平竞争优势，因为大家都会以同样的方式使用它。 但实际发生的是相反的情况。AI 进步的曲线现在几乎是垂直上升的，这意味着如果你在 3～6 个月前把流程固化成制度，你很可能又落后了。再想想那些一年前才开始行动、并自认为已经搞定的公司——它们现在落后得更多。 在我的职业生涯中，从未见过一种技术以这样的速度进步，这也意味着你必须以同样的速度升级自己的预期。我们的大脑天生不是为此设计的，我们的组织变革管理流程当然也不是。 在任何形式的递归式自我改进之下，事情只会变得更狂野。这个时代，你需要不断重新评估那些几个月前还做不到的事，看看它们现在是否已经可以解决。AI 原生公司因此目前拥有明显优势，所以问题就变成了：大公司能否跟上。疯狂的时代。

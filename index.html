The divergence in AI output weirdly may be the highest it's ever been, even though adoption is the broadest it's ever been. Now, the models can do far longer tasks at a time with limited human interaction. The harnesses support agentic work that can take on these larger tasks. The models are trained on vastly better datasets for a growing array of domains so you have to intervene far less. And we have systems that better support working with many agents at once. In just a two year timespan, the upper limits on productivity gains have gone from 30-50% to now likely 5-10X depending on the field. So we've gone from an environment where living at the frontier vs. not paying attention at all could be an order of magnitude difference in your output. This distance is only getting wider from here given the rate of progress we're likely to see with RSI. Crazy times.

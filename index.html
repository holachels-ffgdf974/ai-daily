Moving from chatting back and forth with agents, to having agents that you spin up in the background to do useful work for you is probably on the order of 100X or 1000X more token consumption than what we’re seeing today. Most work right now is essentially rate limited by our ability to process and work with information. Moving data between systems. Reviewing a document. Looking through code for security issues. Processing log data to look for even patterns. Looking up information about a prospective client. Almost all of knowledge work is constrained by these steps, and AI has become a huge unlock to that. So our first step was to use AI to do these individual tasks much faster for us. That was the chatbot phenomenon that we have been experiencing for the past 3+ years. But we can still then only augment the work that we remain in the loop for, and we’re highly limited by our ability to parallelize this work by our own bandwidth and context. But where we’re going is quite different. As AI models continue to improve at incredible rates, we can hand off more and more work to the agent. First it’s letting the agent run with a harder problem. Then it’s giving a few agents multiple parts of a task at once. But we’re not far off from being able to throw an arbitrary number of agents at a problem and just say “come back when you’ve accomplished this goal”. Now imagine that agent swarm for any problem we can think of. Protecting our codebases, data, and systems. QAing every change we make and make sure all our infrastructure is robust and does what it should. Looking through every document for potential risks. Auditing this company with vastly more data reviewed. And on and on. Most of this isn’t even replacing existing work we do, it’s all net new. These agents will get deployed as swarms by people, by workflows, by triggers, and by other agents. And none of this would be possible if the models weren’t getting better, and if the cost of AI didn’t make it affordable. Both of those trends though are playing out. Now the next frontier is just having enough compute. But this is where it’s heading.

从与智能体来回聊天，转向让智能体在后台自行运转、为你完成有用的工作，其 token 消耗量大概是今天的 100 倍甚至 1000 倍。 目前大多数工作本质上都受限于我们处理与运用信息的能力：在系统之间搬运数据、审阅一份文档、排查代码中的安全问题、处理日志数据寻找规律、查询潜在客户的信息。几乎所有知识型工作都受制于这些环节，而 AI 已成为解开这一枷锁的巨大突破口。 所以我们的第一步，是用 AI 帮我们更快地完成这些单项任务。这就是过去三年多我们经历的聊天机器人浪潮。但我们仍然只能增强那些自己还在闭环中的工作，而且受限于自身的带宽与上下文，并行处理的能力极其有限。 但我们要去的地方截然不同。随着 AI 模型以惊人的速度持续进步，我们可以把越来越多的工作交给智能体。先是让它去啃更难的问题，然后是让几个智能体同时处理一个任务的多个部分。而距离我们能够向一个问题投入任意数量的智能体、只说一句「目标达成后再回来」的那一天，已经不远了。 现在想象一下，针对任何我们能想到的问题都部署这样一群智能体：守护我们的代码库、数据与系统；对我们所做的每一次变更做 QA，确保所有基础设施稳定可靠、按预期运行；审查每一份文档中的潜在风险；以远超以往的审查数据量来审计这家公司……诸如此类。其中大部分甚至不是在替代我们现有的工作，而是全新的增量。 这些智能体会由人、由工作流、由触发器、由其他智能体以「蜂群」的形式部署。而这一切的前提，是模型不断变强，以及 AI 的成本变得可负担——这两个趋势都在发生。下一个前沿，只是要有足够的算力。但方向就是这样。

The battle in AI is shaping up to be a battle for context. Everything in AI is about making sure that agents are effective as possible. That effectiveness comes down to whether the agent has the right domain expertise, access to the right context and tools to work with, and are involved in workflow in a way that users can easily interact with, review its work, and incorporate it into the rest of the process. As a consequence, the platforms that are able to capture and leverage the best and most context within their agents — and be able to pick the right models for the task — will be the place where agents do their best work. This is why the applied AI layer has a lot more value than just being an LLM wrapper. The ability to organize the critical knowledge for the work being done, and maintain this knowledge in a governed way where only the right people and agents have access, and the ability to improve the context for agents more and more over time, is critical. Over time, this layer will be able to route work between a variety of models, leveraging frontier intelligence for planning and orchestration and review, and a mix of lower cost models (open or closed) for the large volume of work between these tasks. The applied layer is also in a good position to train and develop its own models as well that are purpose built for their domains. Never good to bet against the bitter lesson, but equally taking a near frontier base model and post training it for just one type of domain work can — in many cases — lower costs or deliver better performance for certain tasks. Finally, this applied layer is also where most of the change management of the workflow will need to occur. This is why FDEs are so important at the applied layer, because this is the point where the customer needs to have specific business problem solved by a particular vendor. Whichever companies can solve that completely in an end-to-end fashion will have the greatest moats. As each day goes on, we’re learning more about what the likely long term market dynamics will look like in AI.
AI 领域的竞争正演变为一场“上下文”之争。AI 的一切都是关于确保代理尽可能高效。这种高效取决于代理是否拥有正确的领域专长、是否能够访问正确的上下文和工具，以及是否以用户易于交互、审查其工作并将其整合到后续流程的方式参与到工作流中。因此，那些能够在代理中捕获和利用最佳、最丰富的上下文，并且能够为任务选择合适模型的平台，将成为代理发挥最佳效能的地方。这就是为什么应用层 AI 的价值远不止是 LLM 包装器。组织完成工作所需的关键知识，以受治理的方式维护这些知识（只有正确的人和代理才能访问），并随着时间的推移不断改进代理的上下文，这些能力至关重要。随着时间的推移，这一层将能够在多种模型之间路由工作，利用前沿智能进行规划、编排和审查，并混合使用较低成本的模型（开源或闭源）来处理这些任务之间的大量工作。应用层也处于有利位置，可以训练和开发针对其领域量身定制的自有模型。永远不要与“苦涩的教训”对赌，但同样，将一个接近前沿的基础模型仅针对一种领域工作进行后训练，在许多情况下可以降低成本或为特定任务提供更好的性能。最后，这个应用层也是大多数工作流变更管理需要发生的地方。这就是为什么 FDE（现场部署工程师）在应用层如此重要，因为这是客户需要特定供应商解决具体业务问题的关键点。任何能够以端到端方式完全解决这一问题的公司都将拥有最深的护城河。每一天，我们都在更多地了解 AI 领域可能的长期市场动态。

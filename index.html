I asked Claude to populate a Civilization-style game map with real data from the US. Opus 5.5 downloaded and parse a ton of public data and 7 hours and 45K lines of code later... it works :) - 31 real data sources including Census, BEA, BLS, EIA, NOAA, USGS, DoD, NPS, FAA, FEMA, CDC... - Real elevation, land cover, GDP, jobs, power plants, mines, ports, airports, bases, parks, universities, freight flows, disasters; all reconciled onto one 25 km hex grid. - The data is converted into Civilization language, e.g. Gold yield is GDP. A city's districts are its real industry specializations: San Jose gets a Campus, Detroit is "Motor city". Its citizens are its real workforce: 1 in 4 San Jose workers are engineers, coders or scientists, against 1 in 14 nationally. Wonders are chosen by published rules, such as national parks by visitor numbers and universities by R&D spending, etc etc. - 19 lenses (income, degrees, faith, disasters…), a City View for 524 metros, and nine advisors plus an Explorer. The Explorer turns any column for any of 3,100+ counties into a table and scatter plot, linked to the map. - Claude decided to skip a few states and territories sry - Built using Claude Code desktop app, Opus 5.5 on medium and turning on ultracode. It's deployed here: https://t.co/QMhmafXMj5 and the "?" has the source link too. I don't exactly know where I was going with this but it's really fun to browse and play with. Possibly it can be expanded or built into a game. I only have half of my weekly Claude quote left though so someone else will have to take over :D. Claude also made a trailer:

我让 Claude 用美国的真实数据填充了一张《文明》风格的游戏地图。Opus 5.5 下载并解析了大量公开数据，7 小时、4.5 万行代码之后……它跑通了 :) - 31 个真实数据源，包括人口普查局、BEA、BLS、EIA、NOAA、USGS、国防部、NPS、FAA、FEMA、CDC…… - 真实的海拔、土地覆盖、GDP、就业、发电厂、矿山、港口、机场、军事基地、公园、大学、货运流量、灾害数据，全部对齐到一张 25 公里的六边形网格上。 - 数据被转译成《文明》的语言，比如「金币产出」就是 GDP。城市的「区域」就是它真实的产业专长：圣何塞得到「校园区」，底特律是「汽车城」。市民就是真实劳动力：圣何塞每 4 个劳动者中就有 1 个是工程师、程序员或科学家，而全国平均是 1/14。「奇观」按公开规则挑选，比如国家公园按游客数量、大学按研发支出等等。 - 19 个观察视角（收入、学历、信仰、灾害……），524 个都会区的城市视图，还有 9 位顾问和一个「探索器」。探索器可以把 3,100 多个县任意一列数据变成表格和散点图，并与地图联动。 - Claude 决定跳过几个州和领地，抱歉啦。 - 用 Claude Code 桌面版构建，Opus 5.5 中等档位，开启 ultracode。 部署在这里：https://t.co/QMhmafXMj5 ，「?」里也有源码链接。我也不太清楚自己到底想做成什么，但浏览和把玩它真的很有趣。也许它可以扩展，或者做成一个游戏。不过我每周的 Claude 额度只剩一半了，所以得有人接手 :D。Claude 还做了一支预告片：

AI native teams will eventually force the surrounding organization to become AI native too. Take software development. coding velocity is way up thanks to AI, while actual shipped products and business impact are unchanged in most companies. That's cos the surrounding system of experimentation tooling, evaluation, launch processes, and gtm are outdated. It's like sticking a Ferrari engine in a sedan and racing it on Indian roads. The engine can rev faster but the car speed is limited by its system and environment. AI native companies were built ground up for this new way of building and shipping. They're also able to shed suboptimal processes faster. Market forces will quickly force all companies to adapt or perish. If you're solving this for your company: map out your product lifecycle and rebuild it ground up - tooling, team structure, gtm, everything.

AI 原生的团队最终会迫使周围的组织也变成 AI 原生。 以软件开发为例。得益于 AI，编码速度大幅提升，但在大多数公司里，真正交付的产品和业务影响却没变。这是因为周围的体系——实验工具、评估、发布流程和 GTM——都已经过时。 这就像把法拉利引擎塞进一辆轿车，然后在印度的路上飙车。引擎转速可以更快，但车速受制于整个系统和环境。 AI 原生公司是从底层为这种新的构建与交付方式而生的。它们也能更快地甩掉次优流程。 市场力量会迅速迫使所有公司适应，否则就被淘汰。 如果你正在为公司解决这个问题：把产品生命周期画出来，然后从零重建——工具、团队结构、GTM，一切。

Coding gives you a window into what the future will look like for a large amount of knowledge work. The first step for most jobs is that we go from doing most of the research, typing, and value creation manually (even if it's using digital tools) to soon coordinating the work that agents are doing. Next, as this step itself becomes redundant or slows down what agents are capable of, we will be giving agents goals to optimize against and they'll go off on and increasingly solve larger problems for us. This may at first blush look like something whose impact gets stuck with coding, but actually a large percentage of knowledge work may eventually just converge with this form factor due to the massive productivity gains that can be generated. Agents will use computers, leverage our existing tools or write new ones, and write code on the fly to solve larger swaths of knowledge work. So even if a task today doesn't seem to be "coding" directly, the task may eventually fold into being coding adjacent. Manufacturing, architecture, life sciences, material sciences, cyber, and on and on. One big hurdle in the way ultimately will be that much of this work remains non-verifiable, so humans will be even more in the loop than they are in coding. But the part that they can hand off will just get bigger and bigger over time as the models are more capable. So in short, pay attention to coding and you'll get a sense for where work is going.

编程为你提供了一个窗口，可以窥见大量知识工作的未来形态。 对大多数工作而言，第一步是从「我们手动完成大部分研究、打字和价值创造」（哪怕用的是数字工具），转变为协调智能体在做的工作。 接下来，当这一步本身变得多余，或者拖慢了智能体的能力时，我们会给智能体设定要优化的目标，它们会自行出发，越来越多地为我们解决更大的问题。 乍看之下，这似乎只是编程领域的事，但实际上，由于能带来巨大的生产力提升，很大比例的知识工作最终都可能收敛到这种形态。智能体会使用计算机，利用我们现有的工具或编写新工具，并即时写代码来解决更大范围的知识工作。 所以，即使某项任务今天看起来并不直接是「编程」，它最终也可能变成与编程相邻的事。制造业、建筑、生命科学、材料科学、网络安全，等等等等。 最终的一大障碍在于，很多这类工作仍然不可验证，因此人类在其中的参与度会比编程更高。但随着模型能力增强，他们能交出去的部分只会越来越大。 简而言之，关注编程，你就能感受到工作的未来走向。

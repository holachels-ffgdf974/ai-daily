Dusting off this tweet from April because this gap in shared understanding of LLM capability is *widening*. It's now less "two groups of people speaking past each other" and more a sharp funnel. - Napkin math somewhere around 6B people (~75% of the population) have barely come in contact with LLMs at all. - Around 1-2B (~20%) are casual and infrequent users of free-tier ChatGPT-like products. This group sees derpy chatbots and treats them a bit like a better Google search, a writing aid, or etc. Maybe an agent tries to book you a flight. I have non-tech friends who (reasonably, imo) say they have not much use for it at all. Even many professionals outside of math&code are in this tier. For example, execs and many other functions spend a lot of their time talking to other people, so while they understand what is happening intellectually, it is still second-hand and a bit abstract. - Now we get to professional use of frontier-grade LLMs in math&code. Somewhere around 20M people (0.2%) see first-hand that large, complex projects that used to take them weeks/months can now be completed by agents with a prompt. Building apps, copying apps, translating apps, decompiling apps from binaries... This has all happened very quickly and recently - less than 1 year ago, I was writing code manually by hand, typing memorized computer code commands into a code editor character by character, occasionally pressing Tab to autocomplete a little chunk of code. - And finally we get to the ~5,000 people (~0.00006%) with access to frontier-grade systems internally. The external world has seen the preview. It looks like swarms of thousands of agents collaborating over weeks on software mega projects: minting zero days, running cyber attacks and defenses at machine speeds, discovering new science, advancing the frontier of mathematics. Things that would have taken top professionals in the industry years of work. Meanwhile, human review and comprehension are starting to fall behind. For example, people are still involved in the "archeology" of the OpenAI-HF incident from many months ago. Mathematicians may be poring over the 722 manuscripts on frontier mathematics for a while. The funnel is driven by a combination of factors: 1. The impact scales with ambition, problem size, and horizon. A question with a paragraph answer barely stresses the system. You need a reservoir of big, difficult problems that you really care about. This aspect drives the consumer / professional dimension of the funnel. 2. The jaggedness of the system (which I have written about a lot separately). Capability peaks in domains that are digital, verifiable and economically valuable. This is because LLM capability emerges from reinforcement learning on verifiable rewards on a curated environment mixture driven by revenue potential. This aspect primarily drives the area (e.g. math&code) dimension of the funnel. 3. Access. Free-tier, paid-tier, internal. So this is the weirdness of the moment. The general public has mostly not interacted with these systems. When they have, it looks like a derpy chatbot. The majority of professionals still see only a modest uplift. And a small sliver of professionals are experiencing the vertigo of the curve going vertical. And it is all happening at the same time.

把这条四月的推文重新翻出来，是因为关于 LLM 能力的认知鸿沟正在*不断拉大*。它现在已不再是"两拨人各说各话"，而更像一个陡峭的漏斗。 - 粗略估算，大约 60 亿人（约占总人口 75%）几乎从未接触过 LLM。 - 大约 10 到 20 亿人（约 20%）是免费版 ChatGPT 类产品的轻度、偶尔用户。这个群体看到的是有点呆萌的聊天机器人，把它当作一个更好用的谷歌搜索、写作助手之类的工具。也许偶尔让 Agent 帮忙订张机票。我有一些非技术圈的朋友（在我看来相当合理）说他们根本用不太上这东西。即便是数学与编程之外的大量专业人士也处于这一层。比如高管和许多其他职能的人，大部分时间都在跟人打交道，所以虽然他们在理智上明白正在发生什么，但那仍是二手信息，有些抽象。 - 接下来是数学与编程领域对前沿级 LLM 的专业使用。大约 2000 万人（0.2%）亲眼见证：过去需要数周甚至数月才能完成的大型复杂项目，如今一个提示词就能让 Agent 搞定。构建应用、复制应用、翻译应用、从二进制文件反编译应用……这一切都发生得极快、极近——不到一年前，我还在手动写代码，一个字符一个字符地把记在脑子里的代码敲进编辑器，偶尔按一下 Tab 补全一小段。 - 最后是约 5000 人（约 0.00006%），他们能在内部接触到前沿级系统。外部世界只看到了预告片。那景象像是成千上万个 Agent 组成的集群，在数周时间里协作推进软件超级项目：铸造零日漏洞、以机器速度发起和防御网络攻击、发现新科学、推进数学前沿。这些工作若由业界顶尖专家来做，需要数年时间。与此同时，人类的审核与理解能力开始跟不上。例如，人们至今仍在为几个月前 OpenAI-HF 事件做"考古"。数学家们可能还要花很长时间去研读那 722 篇前沿数学手稿。 这个漏斗由多重因素共同驱动： 1. 影响力随雄心、问题规模和视野而放大。一个只需一段话就能回答的问题几乎无法给系统带来压力。你需要储备大量你真正在意的、又大又难的问题。这一因素驱动了漏斗中"消费者／专业人士"这一维度。 2. 系统的锯齿状特性（我另外写过很多）。能力在数字化、可验证且具经济价值的领域达到峰值。这是因为 LLM 的能力源自对可验证奖励的强化学习，而环境组合又由收入潜力所驱动。这一因素主要驱动漏斗中"领域"（如数学与编程）这一维度。 3. 访问权限。免费层、付费层、内部层。 这就是当下这种诡异局面的由来。普通大众大多还没跟这些系统打过交道。即便打过，看到的也只是个呆萌聊天机器人。大多数专业人士仍只感受到温和的提升。而极小一撮专业人士，正体验着曲线垂直上升带来的眩晕。而这一切，正同时发生。

This is a good way to understand how early we actually are in AI diffusion right now. The vast majority of people are using AI as a chatbot for simple interactions that approximate next-generation search. This is super useful but dramatically underrepresents the full capability of AI. On the complete other end of the spectrum, agent swarms represent possibly the ultimate form factor of what agents will do inside of our workflows. Math, cyber, bio, materials science, software engineering, finance, and any other field that has valuable tasks that can be executed in parallel where compute can be applied to accelerate work all have this characteristic. Less than a fraction of a fraction of the population uses AI in this way right now, due to a mix of the capabilities of models relative to the user's domain, the cost of doing these types of workflows today, the harnesses available to do this style of AI work, and the availability (or lack thereof) of frontier models to the general public. There will also be a huge amount of change management necessary to get most areas of work -beyond software and AI research- to benefit from this style of agents. Companies need to upgrade their workflows, data needs to integrated and migrated into modern platforms, and companies will need to work through compliance, governance, and security frameworks to pull it off. But this all represents opportunity right now. A lot of this is where the applied layer of AI will really shine in the enterprise because of the domain specificity of this type of work. You can get an early sense of where things are going and the opportunity ahead by seeing through the funnel that Andrej lays out.

这是理解我们当下 AI 普及程度究竟有多早期的一个好方法。绝大多数人把 AI 当作聊天机器人，用来做近似"下一代搜索"的简单交互。这非常有用，但严重低估了 AI 的完整能力。 而在光谱的完全另一端，Agent 集群或许代表了 Agent 在我们工作流中所能做到的终极形态。数学、网络安全、生物、材料科学、软件工程、金融，以及任何拥有可并行执行、可借助算力加速的有价值任务的领域，都具备这一特征。 目前只有极小极小一部分人在这样使用 AI，原因包括：模型能力相对于用户所在领域的匹配度、当下执行这类工作流的成本、可用于这类 AI 工作的工具框架，以及前沿模型对公众的可及性（或者说缺乏）。 此外，要让软件与 AI 研究之外的多数工作领域从这类 Agent 中获益，还需要大量的变革管理。企业需要升级工作流，数据需要被整合并迁移到现代平台，企业还需要打通合规、治理与安全框架才能实现。 但这一切在当下都代表着机会。这很大程度上正是 AI 应用层在企业中真正大放异彩的地方，因为这类工作具有很强的领域特异性。透过 Andrej 描绘的那个漏斗，你可以早早感知到事情将往哪里去，以及前方存在怎样的机会。

Weekend hack: HackerTube. I was annoyed with YouTube's recommendations so I had Claude filter down 43M comments over 20 years of HN to 6,454 videos that keep being recommended. *Significantly* better viewing experience, ~2 hours with Opus 5.5: - Excellent recs without any logins (state is kept in local browser session) - Thumbnail is a representative frame from middle of the video instead of the super duper A/B test hyper-optimized user uploads - Underlying data from the comment analysis as one zip file, point your Claude at it and ask for your own version - HackerTube Longs ✊ A bit more on the process: I use Claude Code (desktop app for Mac), Opus 5.5 on Medium effort as my default. I only talk to Claude and have it do everything, the only "manual work" I helped with in tandem was in the browser (reminiscent of MenuGen): 1) I had to create some kind of API key from Google Cloud UI to query YouTube for video metadata, 2) I booted up a VPS box for serving and 3) I bought the domain name. Once the box was up I gave Claude the IP and told it update, secure, harden, deploy and optimize the code, and it did the whole thing. Many awesome projects that would have normally taken weeks/months even can now be few-shotted for breakfast. What else are we not thinking of.

Aaron Levie @levie
@box CEO —— 你的业务存在于内容之中。用 AI 释放它。

Increasingly this will be the new job description for a lot of roles, not just coding. We’re clearly moving from a world where agents help us do our work incrementally better or faster, to us helping agents do their work. This turns everyone in one of these positions to that of a manager of a team of agents, instead of an individual contributor with just some added leverage from AI. The biggest areas of impact will be information-heavy jobs, like coding, cybersecurity, legal work, biotech, engineering, and many other fields. And as a result, in these jobs your leverage will begin to be decoupled from the normal ratio of hours spent working to output generated. For essentially the history of knowledge work the main variable you had to play with was hours of work done (assuming fixed level talent/coordination/strategy). Now we’ve separated these variables because agents can parallelize work for us, to in theory execute as much as we need to pull off whatever goal we’re trying to accomplish. The people and organizations that discover this sooner, and orient their operations around it, will see compounding gains in their workflows.

这越来越会成为许多岗位的新职位描述，而不只是编程。我们显然正从"智能体帮我们把工作做得更好或更快"的世界，转向"我们帮智能体完成它们的工作"。 这会让身处这些岗位的每个人，从"借助一点 AI 杠杆的个体贡献者"，变成"一支智能体团队的管理者"。影响最大的将是信息密集型的工作，比如编程、网络安全、法律、生物科技、工程，以及许多其他领域。 因此，在这些工作中，你的杠杆率将开始与传统的"投入工时→产出"比例脱钩。纵观知识工作的历史，你所能调节的主要变量一直是工作时长（假设天赋、协作、战略水平固定）。 现在我们把这些变量解耦了，因为智能体可以为我们并行处理工作，理论上可以执行任意多的任务来实现我们想达成的目标。越早发现这一点、并围绕它重构运营的人和组织，将在工作流中获得复利式的收益。

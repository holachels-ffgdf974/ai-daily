If you are a product leader, here is a simple exercise: draw out your product lifecycle. e.g. market understanding -> design -> development -> GTM. Chances are your PMs spend more time in some stages than others - because of the nature of your product, your org or their comfort zones. This is a handoff-heavy, lossy process. Now ask yourself: using AI, how much deeper can PMs go on every stage? PMs should be able to - build a much deeper, broader understanding of users and the market - iterate fast from ideas to working prototypes - explore the codebase to study technical constraints and tradeoffs - run their own analytics and investigate questions as they emerge - understand the GTM funnel deeply enough to shape product strategy Now, this is not about PMs replacing every role. As the function that spans the entire lifecycle, this is about raising the bar on the PM role, so they combine their breadth with much greater depth.
如果你是产品负责人，这里有个简单的练习：把你的产品生命周期画出来。 比如：市场理解 → 设计 → 开发 → GTM。 大概率你的 PM 在某些阶段花的时间明显多于其他阶段——原因可能是产品性质、组织架构，或者只是他们的舒适区。 这是一个交接密集、信息损耗严重的过程。 现在问自己：借助 AI，PM 在每个阶段能深入多少？ PM 应该能够： - 对用户和市场建立更深、更广的理解 - 从想法快速迭代到可运行的原型 - 深入代码库，研究技术约束与取舍 - 自己做数据分析，问题一出现就能追查 - 深刻理解 GTM 漏斗，从而塑造产品战略 这不是说 PM 要取代所有角色。作为贯穿整个生命周期的职能，重点是抬高 PM 这个角色的门槛，让他们把广度与更大的深度结合起来。

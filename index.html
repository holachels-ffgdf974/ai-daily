One thing the engineering community will (re)discover is that every program can be ① hardened (edge cases squashed, inputs tightened, errors handled) and ② optimized (profiled, benchmarked, rewritten) basically ad infinitum. There are real costs in every direction: time, attention, opportunity. You can literally explore input spaces that will never be hit or optimize performance of something that will never be used ("you have no users".) Even in an infinite token world, you need to know when to stop and what tradeoffs to accept. Agents will happily drill no matter what.
工程界将会（重新）发现的一点是：每个程序基本上都可以 ① 被加固（消除边界情况、收紧输入、处理错误），② 被优化（性能分析、基准测试、重写），几乎可以无限进行下去。 每个方向都有真实的成本：时间、注意力、机会。你完全可能去探索永远不会被触及的输入空间，或优化永远不会被用到的东西的性能（“你根本没有用户”）。即使在 token 无限的世界里，你也需要知道何时停下、接受哪些取舍。而 Agent 无论何时都会乐此不疲地继续钻下去。

What should an ambitious software engineer do today? Ten years ago, being a great software engineer gave you an unusual advantage: a rare skill you could apply to almost any industry. You could build a payments company, improve how hospitals operated, or create a new way for people to communicate. The same underlying ability opened doors into wildly different businesses. Software was eating the world, and knowing how to build it gave you a seat at the head of almost every table. “Get really good at software engineering” was remarkably versatile advice. You could acquire the skill first and figure out where to apply it later. AI is making the ability to produce working software much more widely available. People who couldn’t have built a product a few years ago can now get surprisingly far. Experienced engineers can attempt projects that previously required a team. Great engineering is still rare. But “I can build it” is becoming a weaker answer to why you, specifically, have an advantage. The way I see it, there are three paths. 1. Go work on the thing that’s eating software Help create the technology that is making software engineering more accessible. That could mean AI research, or the infrastructure, systems, and tools needed to make new capabilities useful. Find problems where technical ability remains a serious constraint, and where solving them expands what other people can do. 2. Find a world-class domain expert and team up Someone who has spent fifteen years in an industry may know where the expensive mistakes happen, why previous attempts to fix them failed, and who would buy a solution. That knowledge is hard to acquire from the outside. At SPC, we’re seeing more people become promising founders without much prior software engineering experience. They bring deep domain knowledge and strong instincts about what needs to exist. These are people worth getting to know. A great engineering partner can see possibilities they haven’t considered and help build something neither person could have imagined alone. 3. Write vanilla code… by shepherding a bunch of agents This is the direction I find least compelling: doing roughly the same work inside a company, except now you supervise coding agents. There will be valuable jobs like this. But if your ambition extends only to completing the same backlog faster, you’re underusing the moment. I’d still get very good at engineering. Then I’d be deliberate about what that ability sits next to: a technical frontier that needs advancing, or deep knowledge of a problem worth solving. Use the fact that you can build more to take responsibility for something bigger.
一个有抱负的软件工程师今天该做什么？ 十年前，成为一名出色的软件工程师会给你一种不寻常的优势：一项几乎可以应用到任何行业的稀缺技能。 你可以创办一家支付公司、改善医院的运营方式，或创造一种人们全新的沟通方式。同一种底层能力，为你打开了通往截然不同行业的大门。 软件正在吞噬世界，而懂得如何构建软件，让你几乎在每一张桌子上都有一个主位。 “把软件工程练到极致”曾是一条极其通用的建议。你可以先掌握这项技能，之后再决定把它用到哪里。 AI 正在让生产可用软件的能力变得广泛得多。几年前根本造不出产品的人，如今也能走得惊人地远。有经验的工程师可以尝试过去需要一整个团队才能完成的项目。 优秀的工程依然稀缺。但“我能把它做出来”作为“为什么偏偏是你有优势”的答案，正变得越来越无力。 在我看来，有三条路。 1. 去做那件正在吞噬软件的事 帮助创造让软件工程变得更易获取的技术。 这可以是 AI 研究，也可以是让新能力变得有用的基础设施、系统和工具。去寻找那些技术能力仍是严重瓶颈、而解决它们能扩展他人能力边界的问题。 2. 找到一位世界级的领域专家并组队 一个在某个行业深耕十五年的人，可能知道昂贵的错误发生在哪里、过去修复它们的尝试为何失败、谁会为解决方案买单。这种知识很难从外部获得。 在 SPC，我们看到越来越多人没有多少软件工程经验，却成为很有潜力的创始人。他们带来了深厚的领域知识和对“什么必须存在”的强烈直觉。 这些人值得去认识。一位出色的工程伙伴能看到他们未曾考虑的可能性，并帮助构建出任何一方单独都无法想象的东西。 3. 通过牧养一群 Agent 来“手写原生代码”…… 这是我觉得最不吸引人的方向：在一家公司里做大致相同的工作，只不过现在你监督的是编程 Agent。 这类工作会有价值。但如果你的抱负仅限于更快地完成同样的待办事项，那你就低估了这个时代。 我仍然会先把工程练得非常好。然后我会刻意选择让这项能力与什么相邻：一个需要推进的技术前沿，或对一个值得解决的问题的深刻理解。 利用你“能构建更多”这一点，去承担更大的责任。
